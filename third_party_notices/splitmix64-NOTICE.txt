SplitMix64 output mixing

The three integer-mixing expressions used by the private handle tables are adapted from
Sebastiano Vigna's 2015 public-domain SplitMix64 implementation:
https://prng.di.unimi.it/splitmix64.c

The generator state and generator API are not included. The finalizer is used
only to distribute monotonic, non-secret handle IDs among private table slots.
